USGP 2021 Podium



Lewis Hamilton (l) and Max Verstappen (r) on the podium at Circuit of the Americas, Austin, following Max’s win in the 2021 United States Grand Prix. Checo Perez, who was third, is obscured here by a Dutch flag waved by one of the many Verstappen fans in attendance.
