THE TAKEAWAY

What this comparison reveals.

Same car, same session, same tires — and yet 0.122 seconds apart. The gap came almost entirely from two corners: Degner 1, where Vettel's aggressive late-brake found a tenth Webber wouldn't risk, and Spoon, where Vettel's willingness to rotate the car earlier let him pick up throttle a fraction sooner.

The hairpin told the other story: the slow, technical section where Webber's cleaner, more conservative style actually clawed time back. Over a lap, Vettel's high-risk, high-commitment approach won the aggregate — which is why he was the faster qualifier that year, and why he sits on pole on Saturday and loses fewer race stints on Sunday.
